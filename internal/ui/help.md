## How to use downloader?

Use arrow keys or hjkl to move the cursor around. Pressing enter to choose the selection.

## Where should I download to?

Technically you can download to anywhere, but a new separated directory is suggested since it's better for your client to build the datas. Having a gaint folder will massively slow the data build process. But it's up to you :)

## How do I ask/suggest/improve something

You can create an issue or pull request on github: <https://github.com/Catizard/Ginger-Downloader>

## What should I do after downloading the packs?

Remove the files have ".download" suffix and uncompress the others.
